Kanto Leche Flan

Description

I modified this leche flan by starting with a daring substitution for vanilla after we completely ran out of stock. Desperate for a replacement, I scanned our shelves and couldn’t find anything with a more pungently strong, sweet aroma than the bottle of kanto wine sitting in our winery. Curiosity got the best of me, so I decided to use it, wondering if this bold, unconventional pour could finally shake things up and breathe new life into my usual, boring recipe.

Ingredients

  • 6 large egg yolks
  • 1 can (300 ml) condensed milk
  • 1 can (370 ml) evaporated milk
  • 3/4 cup white sugar (for caramelizing the mold)
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons sweet “kanto wine” (in place of vanilla extract)

Steps

  1. Melt the white sugar in the llanera or mold over low heat until golden amber, then swirl to coat the base and set aside to cool.
  2. In a bowl, gently whisk together the egg yolks, condensed milk, evaporated milk, and kanto wine without creating excess bubbles.
  3. Strain the custard mixture through a fine sieve into the caramelized mold.
  4. Cover tightly with foil and steam over medium-low heat for 35 to 45 minutes until set.
  5. Let cool, chill in the refrigerator, and invert onto a serving plate to showcase the glossy caramel glaze.
