Creamy Tuna Penne

Description

Creamy tuna penne is my ultimate “staple food” during the holidays—it’s the signature dish my mom faithfully makes every festive season. While it is already delicious when freshly cooked, letting it sit in the refrigerator takes it to a whole new level; that chilled, spicy, and velvety-creamy sensation in your mouth is simply to die for.

Ingredients

  • 250 g penne pasta
  • 1 can (155g–185g) spicy tuna (or regular tuna flakes with chili flakes/fresh chili)
  • 1 cup all-purpose cream
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil or butter
  • Salt, freshly cracked black pepper, and optional grated cheese to taste

Steps

  1. Boil the penne pasta in salted water until al dente, reserving a splash of pasta water before draining.
  2. Saute chopped onion and minced garlic in oil or butter over medium heat until softened and fragrant.
  3. Stir in the tuna (and chili flakes if using plain tuna) and cook for 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Lower the heat, pour in the all-purpose cream, season with salt and black pepper, and simmer gently until velvety and slightly thickened.
  5. Toss in the cooked penne until well coated. Serve immediately while warm, or refrigerate until chilled for an extra-creamy, refreshing bite.
