Caldeo (Fish with Egg Soup)

Description

I love caldeo, it is my ultimate comfort food whenever I get home from a very tiring day. The rich, savory broth is deeply replenishing, soothing my exhaustion with every spoonful and bringing my mind straight back to the warmth and safety of home.

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh white fish or fish steaks, cleaned and cut into serving pieces
  • 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
  • 5 cups water or fish/vegetable broth
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 thumb-sized piece ginger, julienned
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt, ground black pepper, and chopped green onions to taste

Steps

  1. Heat cooking oil in a pot over medium heat and saute garlic, onion, and ginger until fragrant.
  2. Pour in the water or broth and bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Add the fish pieces and simmer until cooked through, seasoning with salt and pepper.
  4. Slowly drizzle in the beaten eggs while gently stirring to form delicate ribbons in the soup.
  5. Top with chopped green onions and serve hot.
